Meghan Gilligan · EDUC 5305G, Ontario Tech University · Extends "A1 Reflective Diary: Week 2"

My written reflection highlights four areas of assessment and evaluation; grades, effort, self-esteem, and AI. This concept map visualizes these areas, how they intersect and identifies: what I used to believe, the research that challenged it, and what I do differently now. I built the map's structure by talking it through with an AI tool first — asking it to help me see where my four assumptions actually connected, rather than treating them as four separate points.
