The Pyramid Ledger

Kibble (2017) uses Miller's Pyramid to show that written tests reach only the lower levels of competence, while authentic performance tasks reach the levels required in real-world settings. This ledger posts the assessments I have experienced, as a student and as an educator, to the level they actually reach. Each entry records what that assessment costs the learner, what it credits them, and which of Villarroel et al.'s (2018) three dimensions of authentic assessment it touches.
