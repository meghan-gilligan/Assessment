The Exchange Ledger

My source ledger asked whether a grade can be verified. This one asks what a grade is worth to the person who receives it. Every mark is posted in two currencies at once: its exchange value, what it buys (a credit, a pass, a rank), and its information value, what it tells a learner about their learning (Williams, 2022). Because a single mark blends achievement with effort and work habits (Brookhart et al., 2016), the second currency is easy to lose. Below are six entries, from Yale's secret Book of Averages to my own classroom. Switch the reading lens, open each entry, and follow where my motivation landed on Ryan and Deci's (2020) continuum.
