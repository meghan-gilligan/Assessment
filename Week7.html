The Audit Ledger

My earlier ledgers recorded what assessment costs and credits learners, and who holds the pen. This one audits my own books. As a sessional instructor, I inherit approved learning outcomes and syllabi, so every assessment I design is an adjustment to an existing ledger. An audit trail records each adjustment and the evidence behind it. Follow mine from the inherited outcome to a finished task, and see which audit controls each step protects: alignment (Biggs, 1996), validity and reliability (CSAI, 2018), and authenticity (Crompton et al., 2023).
