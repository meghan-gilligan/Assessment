Multimodal Artifact, Reflective Diary: Week 5: Assessment of, for, and as Learning

The Pen Ledger

My earlier ledgers asked what assessment costs and credits learners. This one asks who holds the pen. In assessment of learning, the educator records the entry to certify achievement. In assessment for learning, the educator writes entries the learner can use to improve. In assessment as learning, the learner keeps the books, monitoring, judging, and adjusting their own work (Dixson & Worrell, 2016; Schellekens et al., 2021; Yan & Boud, 2022).
